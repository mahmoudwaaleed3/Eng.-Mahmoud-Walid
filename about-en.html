About

I'm an engineer and instructor specialized in AI, with 3+ years of marketing experience, having worked with major brands like Zara, Adidas, and On Time Sports. I hold certifications from Microsoft, Google, IBM, and LinkedIn, and I use that experience to make AI simple for anyone who wants to get into the field without a heavy technical background.

How the journey started

I started in marketing over 3 years ago, working with global and local brands like Zara, Adidas, and On Time Sports, which gave me a very practical understanding of what the market actually needs. As AI tools started taking off, I noticed a real gap between people who wanted to use these tools in their work and the available content, which was often either too theoretical or too technical.

So I invested in myself and earned certified credentials from Microsoft, Google, IBM, and LinkedIn in AI and digital transformation, and built on that to create courses and books that combine a practical understanding of AI tools with direct application in marketing and data analysis, in simple language.

Today I share that content with thousands of followers across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, and I help small business owners and freelancers use AI as a real working tool, not a passing trend.
